Our videos

Explore rain, fan noise, air-conditioner hum, singing bowls, and other peaceful soundscapes. Our black-screen videos minimise visual distractions during listening.

How we use YouTube

Our privately operated publishing tool uses Google's OAuth authorisation and the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the Restwavee Studio channel, check upload status and video duration, and manage uploads securely. The tool is intended for the channel owner's use, not for public sign-in.

For details about Google account permissions and data handling, read our Privacy Policy.
